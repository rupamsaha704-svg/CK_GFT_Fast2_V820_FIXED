The anatomy of Price Movement o
Let’s assume the fundamental truths about price movement;

1) If price is to move UP, it must first move DOWN

2) If price is to move DOWN, it must first move UP

Why this is so? Because the essence of every major player in the financial
markets is to manipulate prices to his own advantage. They trade off the

liquidity (bulk orders) at each level of support and resistance (SnR)
