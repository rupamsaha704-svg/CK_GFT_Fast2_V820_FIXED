What is Daily Bias?
The Daily Bias is your roadmap. It tells you where price is likely to go for the day.
Without it, you're just guessing.
But with IRL & ERL, you gain the skill to anticipate price movements.
Here's the foundation:

Price ALWAYS does 1of 2 things:
Seeks Liquidity (ERL - External Range Liquidity)
Offers Fair Value (IRL - Internal Range Liquidity)
INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL LIQUIDITY
Internal Range Liquidity (IRL) is defined by Fair Value Gap (FVG)
External Range Liquidity (ERL) is defined by Highs and Lows
When External Range Liquidity is taken a FVG (IRL) becomes the next draw
When a FVG is tagged an old low or old high (ERL) becomes the next draw
