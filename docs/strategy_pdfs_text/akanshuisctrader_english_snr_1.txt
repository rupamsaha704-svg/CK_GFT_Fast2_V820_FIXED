HOW TO IDENTIFY 
THE SND (SUPPLY&DEMAND)
• Supply & Demand are confirmation.
• This confirmation will be divided into four (RBD,DBR,RBR,DBD).
• For RBR or DBD, we will make confirmation as a continuation.
• For RBD or DBR, we will make confirmation as a  reversal.
➢RBR means the price want to keep going up.
➢DBD means the price want to keep going down.
➢RBD means the price want to change direction, from up to down.
➢DBR means the price want to change direction, from down to up.
