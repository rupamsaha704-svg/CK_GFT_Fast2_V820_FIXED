What is TGIF? 
TGIF or “Thank God It’s Friday” is a day based algorithmic ICT trading model. It’s a set 
up occurs on Friday and is based on the concept of retracement into the current weekly 
range.
Step/rules TGIF
1. High/low of the week on monday or tuesday [Weekly profile : Classic 
bullish/bearish]
2. Expansive through thursday into HTF PD ARRAY with -2, -2.5, & -4stdv
3. Weekly objective achieved 
4. TGIF setup into the 20-30% of the weekly range
Nb: Stdv = standard deviation [projection movement]
